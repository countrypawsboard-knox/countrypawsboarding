Welcome to Country Paws

Good care. Room to roam. A place to rest.

Country Paws Boarding is a small, home-based dog boarding business in Knox, Indiana.

We board one guest dog at a time, allowing us to focus on your dog's individual needs and routine.

Dogs enjoy outdoor time in our fenced area and covered kennel, then come inside the house to sleep. During bad weather, dogs stay indoors.

Drop-off and pick-up are arranged by appointment throughout the day.

Outdoor Time

What to Bring

Your Dog's Food

Owners must bring all food their dog will need for the entire stay. Country Paws does not provide dog food.

Bowls & Water

Country Paws provides bowls and fresh drinking water.

Comfort Items

You may bring your dog's own bed, blanket, or favorite toys. Please bring any medication with clear written instructions.

Before booking

Boarding Requirements & Policies

Who We Welcome

  • Adult dogs, at least 1 year old.
  • Friendly and harmless dogs.
  • Fully house-trained dogs.
  • Current required vaccinations, with proof provided.
  • No history of aggression toward people or animals.

Behavior Expectations

  • No excessive barking.
  • Mild separation nervousness may be okay.
  • Severe separation anxiety or destructive behavior may make a dog unsuitable.
  • Owners must disclose behavior and medical concerns.
  • No mandatory meet-and-greet.

Reservations

  • One guest dog at a time.
  • Drop-off and pick-up by appointment.
  • No deposit required.
  • Please contact us if plans change.

Medication & Emergencies

  • Owners supply medications and written instructions.
  • An emergency contact and veterinarian information are required.
  • Owners will be contacted if a dog becomes sick or injured.
  • Urgent veterinary care may be arranged if the owner cannot be reached.
