Welcome to Country Paws

More than a place to stay.

Country Paws Boarding is a small, home-based dog boarding business located in Knox, Indiana. Our goal is to provide a comfortable, caring place for your dog while you're away.

We board one guest dog at a time, allowing us to focus on your dog's individual needs and routine.

Drop-off and pick-up are arranged by appointment throughout the day.

How Boarding Works

Room to enjoy the outdoors, with indoor comfort when it's time to rest.

Outdoor Time

Dogs spend time outdoors in our fenced area and covered kennel. The kennel has fencing on all sides, a gate, and a solid metal-sided wall.

Indoor Comfort

Dogs come inside the house to sleep. During bad weather, they stay indoors for comfort and protection.

Personal Attention

What to Bring

Your Dog's Food

Owners must bring all food their dog will need for the entire stay. Country Paws does not supply dog food.

Bowls & Water

Fresh drinking water and food and water bowls are provided by Country Paws.

Bedding & Favorites

You may bring your dog's own bed, blanket, or favorite toys. Bedding can also be provided here.

If your dog takes medication, please bring it with clear written instructions. Owners must also provide feeding instructions.

A safe and comfortable stay

Boarding Requirements & Policies

Who We Welcome

  • Adult dogs, at least 1 year old.
  • Friendly and harmless dogs.
  • Fully house-trained dogs.
  • Dogs must be current on required vaccinations, with proof provided.
  • No history of aggression toward people or animals.

Behavior Expectations

  • No excessive barking.
  • Mild separation nervousness may be okay.
  • Severe separation anxiety or destructive behavior may make a dog unsuitable.
  • Owners must honestly disclose behavior and medical concerns.
  • No mandatory meet-and-greet.

Reservations

  • One guest dog at a time.
  • Drop-off and pick-up by appointment.
  • No deposit required.
  • Please contact us if plans change.
  • At least 48 hours' notice for cancellations to avoid a cancellation fee.

Medication & Emergencies

  • Owners supply all medications and written instructions.
  • An emergency contact is required.
  • Owner's veterinarian information is required.
  • Owners will be contacted if a dog becomes sick or injured.
  • Urgent veterinary care may be arranged if the owner cannot be reached.
